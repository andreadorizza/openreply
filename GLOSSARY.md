# OpenReply

Instagram comment-to-DM campaigns: when someone comments a keyword on a post, the account answers with a DM, and optionally with a public reply.

## Language

**Campaign**:
A rule that answers matching comments on its posts with a DM.
_Avoid_: Automation, flow, auto-responder

**Post**:
A piece of Instagram media that a campaign can listen on: a photo, a carousel or a reel.
_Avoid_: Video, media

**Post trigger**:
Which posts a campaign listens on: chosen posts, any post, or the next reel.
_Avoid_: Trigger scope

**Next reel**:
A post trigger that waits for the first reel posted after it is armed, then keeps that reel as a chosen post.
_Avoid_: Upcoming reel

**Reel**:
A video post.
_Avoid_: Video, clip

**Trial reel**:
A reel that Instagram shows to non-followers first. It is still a post, and it keeps being one after it graduates.
_Avoid_: Test reel

**Graduate**:
For a trial reel, to be shared with the account's followers, by hand or automatically by Instagram.
_Avoid_: Promote, publish
